# Side Deck – Support

Side Deck turns your iPad into an additional control surface for your DJ software.

## Setup

1. Open Side Deck on your iPad and tap "Set up Bluetooth connection" to pair it with your Mac. You can also connect via USB cable.
2. Import the Side Deck mapping file for your DJ software.
3. Load a track and tap a pad – your DJ software should respond right away.
